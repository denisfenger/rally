How it works

Tap. Rally does the rest.

01

Tap to score

Your side is blue, theirs is clay. One tap on the wrist records a point and a haptic confirms it. A wrong tap is one tap from undone.

02

The rules are handled

Games, sets, tiebreaks, serve rotation and side switches follow the rules you picked for the sport: golden point or advantage, best of three or five, rally or side-out scoring.

03

Your phone is the scoreboard

Prop your iPhone or iPad courtside and it mirrors the score as you play. No watch at hand? Score on the phone instead, with the same rules.

Sports

Every net, one app.

Pick a default sport once and set its rules. Add the others whenever you play them; each keeps its own preset, and every new match starts from your defaults. Custom covers any first-to-a-number sport, win by two or not.

Live sharing

Share the score with a 6-digit code.

Followers need no account and nothing to install: the link opens a live scoreboard in any browser, or they enter the code in the Rally app. Following is always free; hosting a share is part of Rally Pro.

Sharing is ephemeral by design. While the match runs, our server holds only the code and the current score, and both are deleted the moment the match ends.

Built to be trusted

Never loses a point.

The watch is the source of truth. A dropped connection never costs a point; the phone and every follower simply catch up when it returns.

Counts as a workout

With your permission, Rally records each match as a workout with heart rate and calories from the watch, so it counts toward your Activity rings. It never takes over a workout you already started in another app.

Local first

Your history lives on your devices, not on our servers. Export it any time, free, in an open format. Heart rate and calories never leave the watch and phone.
