What Rally is

Rally is a score keeper for racket and net sports, made by Fenger Digital GmbH in Germany. You tap on your Apple Watch to record a point and Rally derives games, sets, serve rotation and side switches from the rules of the sport: padel, tennis, pickleball, table tennis, badminton, squash, volleyball, or a custom first-to-a-number game. Your iPhone or iPad doubles as the courtside scoreboard and can score a match itself. Rally Pro adds unlimited on-device history and lets you share a match live with a 6-digit code; following is always free.

Common questions

My purchase did not unlock Pro. Open Rally on your iPhone, go to Settings and use Restore purchases in the Rally Pro section. If Pro still does not unlock, email us from an address we can answer and we will sort it out with Apple's records.

I want my data removed. Rally keeps your matches only on your devices. Deleting the app removes them, and Settings > Reset app removes them while keeping the app. The one record that lives elsewhere, the purchase record at RevenueCat, is deleted on request by email. Details are in the privacy policy.

I want my match history as a file. Settings > Export history writes your full history in an open format, free, whether or not you subscribe.

The share link says there is no live match. A share lives only while the host's match is running. When the host ends the match or stops sharing, the code stops working, and sharing again gives a new code.

Where are my heart rate and calories? They are recorded through Apple Health on the watch and stay there. Rally never uploads them, and you manage or delete them in the Health app.
