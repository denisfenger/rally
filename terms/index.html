2. The service

Rally tracks the score of racket and net sports from your wrist or phone and derives games, sets, serve rotation and side switches from the rules you pick. The following stays free: scoring and match tracking, the courtside scoreboard, following a shared match as a viewer, a 30-day match history and the full export of your history at any time. Your data is never held hostage: export works regardless of any subscription.

Rally Pro adds unlimited on-device history and the ability to host live sharing so others can follow your match.

4. Your content

The scores and rule settings you record remain yours and remain on your device. Rally has no field for names or notes; a shared match shows only the score, the sport and who serves. Should a future version let you enter names, you are responsible for what you type, especially for other people's names when you share a match. Keep it fair, the way you would on court.

5. Live sharing ground rules

Live sharing is ephemeral by design: the server holds only a join code and the current score while a match is actively shared, and deletes them at match end. Do not abuse the sharing service, for example by automated mass requests, guessing codes or using it for anything other than following a match. We may rate limit, block or end abusive use.

Scoring works entirely offline. The live sharing service is provided with reasonable care but without a guaranteed uptime; a dropped connection never loses your score, because your device is the source of truth.
